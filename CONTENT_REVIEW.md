# Business review before publication

The frontend is implemented and locally tested. These content decisions need owner review before the website is published:

1. **Portfolio wording.** Certanika Lite and Tanikkai are presented as software platforms. Certanika retains its existing URL but is identified separately as a certification and assessment offering. Confirm the portfolio relationship and approved descriptions. All three inherited public destinations returned HTTP 200 during verification; that does not establish business ownership or classification.
2. **Practice notes.** Approve the existing governance, engineering, incident-response, privacy, and AI-governance copy for public use. It is labeled general practice guidance, with no invented authors, dates, or research claims. Incomplete and administrative test blog content from the reference was excluded.
3. **Legal wording.** Review the carried-forward Terms and the website-specific Privacy notice, particularly how the production hosting provider handles logs, enquiries, and retention. No legal-compliance certification is claimed.
4. **Asset rights.** Confirm ownership/permission for the supplied founder photos and logo, and retained licenses for inherited section photography. The reference identifies Pexels as its stock source but does not include per-photo receipts.
5. **Company details.** Confirm that the inherited founder roles, bios, phone, email, office address, delivery locations, business hours, mission, and vision remain current.

Unsupported client statistics, awards, partner logos, certification badges, case-study results, and testimonials are excluded from the rendered website. No new factual achievement or named client claim was invented.

## Contact delivery

The original working project's contact flow prepares a `mailto:` draft. That behavior is preserved and clearly explained. The website itself does not submit or store the enquiry. A direct delivery backend, CRM, SMTP integration, subscriptions, and analytics were not configured in the working project and have not been invented or copied from the reference's separate backend. Direct submission would require a separately configured endpoint, validation, abuse controls, and privacy review.

## Publication

No deployment, DNS change, production database change, or message sending was performed. The site can be reviewed locally before publication.
